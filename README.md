# Neuromorphic Computing Research

Research project exploring energy-efficient computational architectures inspired by biological neural systems, with a long-term focus on resource-constrained embedded and edge computing.

> **Research status:** Ongoing  
> **Publication status:** Research and manuscript preparation in progress

---

## Research Motivation

Conventional computing architectures can face significant energy and resource constraints when deployed at the edge.

This research explores alternative approaches to neural computation with the objective of investigating whether useful computational behavior can be achieved with substantially lower resource requirements.

The work builds upon previous research in ultra-low-power neuromorphic electronics and extends the investigation toward computational models suitable for embedded systems.

---

## Research Direction

The project investigates:

- Neuromorphic computation
- Ultra-low-power artificial neurons
- Energy-efficient neural architectures
- Mathematical modeling of neural behavior
- Embedded neural computation
- Resource-constrained inference
- Scalable neural network architectures

The research is intentionally developed from first principles, combining electronic systems, mathematical modeling and embedded software.

---

## Methodology

The research follows an iterative engineering and scientific methodology:

1. Define the computational problem.
2. Establish mathematical models.
3. Develop experimental implementations.
4. Validate behavior using controlled problems.
5. Analyze computational and energy requirements.
6. Investigate scalability to larger architectures.
7. Document findings and limitations.
8. Prepare validated results for scientific publication.

---

## Current Progress

The current development includes:

- Artificial neuron research and modeling.
- Mathematical formulation of neural behavior.
- Custom neural network implementation from first principles.
- Successful validation using the XOR classification problem.
- Investigation of activation functions.
- Development toward configurable multi-layer neural architectures.

The research is ongoing and results are being progressively evaluated.

---

## Expected Impact

The long-term objective is to investigate computational architectures capable of bringing more efficient intelligence to resource-constrained devices.

Potential application areas include:

- Edge AI
- Embedded intelligence
- IoT devices
- Ultra-low-power electronics
- Neuromorphic computing
- Energy-efficient inference

---

## Research Protection Notice

This repository presents the research motivation, direction, methodology and selected non-sensitive progress.

Core implementation details, unpublished experimental data, proprietary architectures and other information that could compromise ongoing research are intentionally excluded while the work is being prepared for publication.
